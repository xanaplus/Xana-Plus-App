---
name: Product card controls
description: User-approved add-button style and alignment intent for product cards.
---

The user chose a “+ Add” pill, rather than a circular plus button, for product cards. Adjacent price and add rows should line up despite optional pack labels or differing product-name lengths.

**Why:** the user pointed out misaligned controls on the Red Onions and Garlic cards and selected the recommended pill style.

**How to apply:** retain this style when adding or redesigning product cards, without changing cart, stock or prescription behavior.
