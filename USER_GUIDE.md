# XanaPlus User Guide

## What is XanaPlus

XanaPlus is the Xana LIFE mobile app for buying groceries, fresh produce, deli, pharmacy, liquor and wholesale goods, delivered across Nairobi. What makes it different from an ordinary grocery app is that a **licensed pharmacy sits inside the same basket** — one order can hold avocados and prescription medicine together, with a real pharmacist checking anything that needs one before it ships.

You pay by M-Pesa, cash on delivery or card, track your order from pick to doorstep, and earn Xana Club points on everything you buy — groceries and pharmacy alike.

## Getting started

You don't need an account to browse — open the app and shop straight away. An account is only needed when you place an order.

1. Enter any Kenyan mobile number (+254 7XX XXX XXX).
2. Enter the 6-digit code sent by SMS to that number.
3. You're signed in — your name, phone number (partly hidden for privacy) and Xana Club tier show on your Profile.

You stay signed in until you sign out, and a session that's been idle for 30 days signs out automatically.

## Browsing and ordering

- **Six departments** — Groceries, Pharmacy, Deli, Retail, Wholesale, Liquor — each one tap away from Categories.
- **Search** across every department at once, with filters for department, price, stock and express delivery, and sorting by relevance, price or newest.
- **Scan a barcode** to jump straight to a product's page.
- **Bulk pricing** applies automatically once you buy enough of an item — the lower unit price and your saving show in the basket.
- **Out-of-stock choice** — at checkout you decide up front what happens if something you ordered isn't available: replace it, refund it, or have your shopper call you first. If it comes up while you're being shopped for, you get asked again in the moment, with the specific item named.
- **Reorder** anything from your order history in one tap.

## Paying

- **M-Pesa** — the default. Confirm at checkout and a payment prompt (STK push) goes straight to your phone; enter your M-Pesa PIN there. XanaPlus never sees or stores that PIN.
- **Cash on delivery** — pay the rider when your order arrives.
- **Card** — Visa or Mastercard, through a secure hosted gateway.
- If a payment doesn't go through, the app tells you why (timed out, cancelled, or insufficient balance) and confirms plainly that **nothing was charged**. You can resend the prompt or switch payment method; after three failed attempts you're offered another method automatically.
- **Xana Club points** can be applied at checkout to lower what you pay — 10 points = KES 1, with quick presets or a "use maximum" option.

## Pharmacy

- **Upload a prescription** — photograph it (the app checks the photo is readable), say who it's for, and a licensed pharmacist reviews it — typically under 30 minutes, 7am–10pm. You're notified as it moves from received → with pharmacist → dispensed → ready.
- **Repeat medicines** — your regular prescriptions are kept on file with the prescriber, last-dispensed date and refills remaining, so reordering is one tap. An expired prescription is flagged and blocked until renewed.
- **Ask a Pharmacist** — a free, confidential chat with a named, registered pharmacist, by WhatsApp or in-app.
- **Clinical services** — book a blood pressure check, blood glucose/HbA1c screening, vaccination, cholesterol panel, malaria/typhoid test or a full consultation at a branch, with a time slot and price shown upfront.
- Every pharmacy screen shows our Pharmacy and Poisons Board (PPB) licence, and a consultation always states plainly that it isn't a substitute for emergency care — call 999 for that.
- Your prescriptions and medicine names are private: they're never shown on a shared or summary screen, and pharmacy spending appears elsewhere only as one total, never itemised.

## Delivery and tracking

- Choose a **delivery slot** or **pickup** from Syokimau or Ruiru at checkout; delivery is free from both branches.
- Track your order live through four stages — Received, Shopping, Out for delivery, Delivered — with the current stage always clear at a glance.
- If an item turns out to be unavailable while you're being shopped for, you're asked in the moment: replace it, refund it, or have your shopper call you.
- **Returns** — from a delivered order, request a return on any item, give a reason, and choose to be refunded to M-Pesa or as Xana Club account credit (which carries a small bonus).
- Your order history lists every order with a filter for active, delivered or cancelled.

## Xana Club rewards

Every order — groceries and pharmacy alike — earns Xana Club points, shown as their own line at checkout so your savings are never folded into another figure. Points climb you through Bronze, Silver, Gold and Platinum tiers, and can be redeemed against a future order (10 points = KES 1). Your Profile shows a running breakdown of savings and spending by department and by month.

## Your account

- **Addresses** — add as many delivery addresses as you like: drop a pin at your gate, and the app tells you straight away if it's inside our delivery zone. Label each one Home, Work or Other, and set a default for one-tap checkout.
- **Notifications** — a master switch, plus fine-grained control over order updates, pharmacy alerts, and promotions — turn off what you don't want without losing the alerts that matter.
- **Privacy** — change your sign-in number, control whether your order history is shared with your pharmacist, and review how your data is handled, all from Account & Privacy.

## Getting help

From Profile, **Help & Support** connects you to your branch. For anything pharmacy-related, **Ask a Pharmacist** is free and confidential. In a medical emergency, call 999 — XanaPlus's pharmacist chat is not a substitute for emergency care.
