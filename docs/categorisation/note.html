How the Xana Plus app categorises products

For William, website team · 17 October 2026

The app sorts every product by one Business Central field: the Inventory Posting Group. Each group maps to an app department and shelf. For example, Panadol is in OVER THE COUNTER, so it shows under Pharmacy → Over the Counter. Item Category Code and Gen. Prod. Posting Group are not used. We suggest the website uses the same grouping, so a product sits in the same place on both.

Pharmacy photo rule (from the pharmacy team). Items in GENERAL, CHRONIC and CONTROLLED must be listed without photos, on the app and on the website. They can still be sold. Retail also lists some everyday grocery and household shelves (cooking oil, cleaning, soft drinks, staples) so retail shoppers find them in one place. BC has no "Household" or "Beauty" department of its own; the app builds both from the groups above.

The exceptions

  • Deli goes by item number: any item whose number starts with DEL is Deli, whatever its group.
  • Wholesale is picked by pack words in the name: "box", "outer", "carton", "ctn", "bale", "crate".
  • Some shelves split one group by name words: Liquor into Beer & Cider ("lager", "stout"…) and Wine & Spirits ("whisky", "gin"…); the Deli meat and cheese counters by "sausage", "bacon", "cheese"…
  • Prescription needed: group CONTROLLED, or an item number starting with CONTR. 18+: group WINES & SPIRITS.
  • What shows at all: active, price above zero and show_on_website (the catalogue view). Shelves list in-stock items only; search lists everything, in stock first.

For the website

  • If the website uses the same grouping, the mapping lives in the app repo: BC_GROUP_CATEGORY in src/data/live-catalogue.ts and the shelves in verticals, src/data/catalog.ts. We are happy to move it into a shared table if that is easier for you.
  • If the website already groups differently, please tell us how, so the two can match.
  • A product in the wrong place is fixed in BC by changing its Inventory Posting Group; the next morning's sync (06:30) moves it. The back office is already working through known cases, for example Ariel and Golden Fry oil in FOODSTUFFS.
