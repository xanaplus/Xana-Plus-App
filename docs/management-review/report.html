Xana Plus
What the App Does

A screen-by-screen review of the customer app, for management

23 September 2026

Summary

Xana Plus lets a Xana customer order groceries and pharmacy items from their phone, pay by M-Pesa, and follow the order to their door. Every customer-facing screen is now designed and wired: all 53 required screens run on a phone with sample data built in.

There is no system behind the screens yet. Products, stock, payments and orders are placeholders, so nothing a customer does is saved or reaches a store. The backend is the next phase of work.

We ask management for feedback on the screens shown here.

How to read this document

Each picture below is a real screen from the app, captured on 23 September 2026 at phone size. The customer in every picture is Amina Odhiambo, a sample Gold member. The prices, stock and orders are sample data, not live figures.

When this document says a screen is built, it means the screen works on a phone with sample data. It does not mean the screen is connected to a store, a payment system or a pharmacist. Those connections are the backend, covered in “What is not done yet”.

The customer journey at a glance

A customer can browse without an account and signs in only to place an order. From there the journey splits into shopping and pharmacy, and meets again at delivery.

Browse & search→Basket→Checkout→Sign in (phone + SMS code)
→Pay by M-Pesa→Track order→Delivered or return
Pharmacy→Same basket·Prescription → pharmacist review

The pharmacy shares the same basket as groceries. One order can hold avocados and prescription medicine, with a pharmacist checking anything that needs one before it ships.

Screen by screen

The screens are grouped in the order a customer meets them: shopping, paying, receiving the order, the pharmacy, and their account.

1. Shopping

No account is needed to shop. A customer can open the app, browse every department, search and fill a basket before giving any details.

Home

The week’s deals, the customer’s branch (Syokimau) and the main categories. Search and the barcode scanner sit at the top.

Categories

Six departments one tap apart: Retail, Wholesale, Deli, Pharmacy, Groceries and Liquor. Wholesale shows bulk carton pricing.

Search

One search covers every department, with filters for price, stock and express delivery. The basket total stays visible.

Product

Price, stock, delivery speed and details, with a quantity selector and an add-to-basket button.

Barcode scan

Point the camera at a packaged product to open its page or compare prices. In this version the scan is simulated; it does not use the camera yet.

2. Basket and payment

Basket

Items, slot and total. Bulk pricing applies on its own and the saving is shown (here, KES 240). The customer also sets what happens if an item is out of stock.

Checkout

Address, delivery slot and payment method: M-Pesa, cash on delivery or card. Signed-in members can also spend Xana Club points, at 10 points to KES 1.

Sign in, at checkout

Asked for only at Place Order: a mobile number and a six-digit SMS or WhatsApp code, no password. Today any number works and the code is 123456.

M-Pesa prompt

A payment prompt goes to the customer’s phone, where they enter their M-Pesa PIN. Xana Plus never sees the PIN. In this version the prompt is a timer; no money moves.

Payment not completed

Says why the payment failed and that nothing was charged. The customer can resend, pay on delivery or switch method. Most shopping apps leave this screen out.

3. Orders and delivery

My orders

Every order with its status, branch, items and total. Active orders can be tracked; delivered ones reordered in one tap.

Live tracking and substitutions

Four stages: Received, Shopping, Out for delivery, Delivered. If an item is out of stock, the customer is asked in the moment to choose a replacement, a refund or a call. The “Advance status (demo)” button exists only for presentations.

Item on hold

If the shopper cannot reach the customer, the item is held. Nothing is replaced or removed until the customer chooses, so they are never charged for something they did not pick.

Return and refund

From a delivered order, pick the items, give a reason and choose a refund to M-Pesa or as Xana Club credit.

4. Pharmacy

The pharmacy is what sets Xana Plus apart from a plain grocery app. Every pharmacy screen shows the Pharmacy and Poisons Board licence. Medicine names never appear on shared or summary screens.

Pharmacy home

Upload a prescription, consult a doctor, view prescriptions, ask a pharmacist. “Rx Required” items need a pharmacist’s approval before they ship. Prescriptions and pharmacist chat need sign-in.

Upload a prescription

Photograph it, say who it is for and confirm it is valid. A licensed pharmacist reviews it, typically within 30 minutes, 7am to 10pm.

My prescriptions

Received, With pharmacist, Dispensed, Ready. Regular medicines are kept on file with refill dates; expired ones are flagged.

Order a repeat

Tick the medicines to reorder and send them to the pharmacist. Expired prescriptions are blocked until renewed. Refill reminders can be switched on.

Ask a pharmacist

A free, private consultation with a named, registered pharmacist. The customer may share current medicines so interactions can be checked.

Pharmacist chat

By WhatsApp or in the app, and always marked “not for emergencies, call 999”. The replies shown are sample text; no pharmacist is connected yet.

Clinical services

Six in-branch services with prices upfront, including a blood pressure check (KES 150), blood glucose screening (KES 350) and vaccinations.

Book a service

Pick a branch, a date and a time, then confirm. Cancellation is free up to one hour before.

5. Account and rewards

Profile

Xana Club tier and points with progress to the next tier; orders, prescriptions, addresses, notifications and privacy below.

Add an address

Drag a pin to the gate and see at once whether it is inside the delivery zone. The map is a placeholder drawing, not a live map.

Savings and spending habits

Opened from the Savings figure on Profile. It shows how much Xana has saved the customer and where their money goes, over 3 months, 6 months or all time. In the sample, Amina saved KES 1,010 on KES 6,090 spent across 3 orders, or 14% of her basket value.

  • How you saved: savings by source: bulk pricing (KES 240), promotions (KES 320) and free delivery (KES 450).
  • Where it went: spending by department, with each one's share of the total.
  • Pharmacy shown as a total: medicines are never itemised here, so a shared phone does not reveal them.
  • Month by month: a simple bar chart of spending per month.
  • Xana Club: points balance, points earned in the period and distance to the next tier.
  • Your regulars: the items the customer buys most, each with a one-tap Reorder.
