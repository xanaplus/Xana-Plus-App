# Where these images come from

The pictures in this folder came from the XanaPlus design file in Figma (G4iVN6s7cWFkkl2oCXuhb8), which was made from Google Stitch mock-ups. They are **illustrations, not photos of Xana shops or real Xana stock**, and some show made-up pack designs (for example "VITA-BOOST"). The app uses them only for category tiles and section cards, never as the photo of an item for sale; item photos come from the POS image API.

Used on the new Home (19 Nov 2026): `cheese-counter.jpg` (Shop now card), `omron-m2-bp-monitor.jpg` (Pharmacy door), `fresh-produce.jpg` (Fresh food door), `vitamins-supplements.jpg` (Wellness door).

To replace with real Xana photos once Marketing supplies them (asked 19 Nov 2026).
